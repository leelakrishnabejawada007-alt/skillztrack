Transparent by design

Rule-based, not a black box

Every recommendation comes from simple rules you can read and check by hand. Bigger gaps on more important skills come first, prerequisites come before advanced topics, and each milestone explains why it was suggested.

gap = max(0, required − yours)priority = gap × importancereadiness = Σ min(yours, required) × importance ÷ Σ required × importance
