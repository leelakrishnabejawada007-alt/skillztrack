{{ icon("info") }} How is this calculated? (rule-based, no AI) {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
  • Gap = max(0, required level − your level).
  • Priority = gap × importance (1 nice to have, 2 important, 3 core).
  • Mastered = gap 0. Missing = verified level 0 or not yet verified. To improve = verified level 1 or more but below the target.
  • Your level = floor of your verified 0-5 score, built from graded assignments (see your profile); self-ratings are never used.
  • Readiness = sum of (min(yours, required) × importance) ÷ sum of (required × importance).
