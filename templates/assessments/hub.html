{{ icon("info") }} How your score is calculated (no self-rating involved) {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
  • Every assignment can prove up to a ceiling level: quizzes up to 3, rubric-checked work up to 4, auto-tested code up to 5.
  • Attempt score = ceiling × how much of the tests/rubric you earned. Scoring 80% counts as mastery of that tier.
  • Your skill score (0–5) is your best assignment result. One assignment can prove at most 3.5; levels 4 and 5 need evidence from at least two assignments.
  • Beginner and Easy tasks allow 10 free retries with no penalty. Harder tasks allow {{ max_attempts }}; from the 3rd attempt each retry is worth 15% less, so practise first.
  • Confidence is low with one assignment, medium with two and high with three or more including auto-tested code.
